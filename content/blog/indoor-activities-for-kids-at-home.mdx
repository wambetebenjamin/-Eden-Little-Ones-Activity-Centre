Nairobi's rainy season often keeps little ones indoors for days at a time. The
good news: you don't need a big budget or a playroom to keep children aged 2 to
12 engaged, learning and happy at home.

## 1. Build a blanket fort reading corner

Pile cushions and blankets into a cosy corner and rotate in a few picture books.
Young children love "disappearing" into their own little world while listening
to a story.

## 2. Kitchen-safe sensory bins

Fill a basin with dried beans, rice or lentils and hide small toys inside for
toddlers to "dig" for. It's a simple way to build fine motor skills.

## 3. Living-room obstacle course

Use cushions, chairs and string to build a simple course your child can crawl
under, step over and jump across — great for burning energy safely indoors.

## 4. Paper plate crafts

Paper plates, crayons and a little glue go a long way — turn them into masks,
suns or clocks.

## 5. Family dance breaks

Put on some Afrobeat and take five-minute dance breaks between homework or
chores. It keeps the whole family moving and smiling.
